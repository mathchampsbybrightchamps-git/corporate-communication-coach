# Feature Documentation: Corporate Jargon Radar & 308-Term Dictionary

## 1. Feature Overview
The **Corporate Jargon Radar & Dictionary** is a comprehensive reference module containing **308 corporate jargon terms** across General Business, Strategy, Engineering, Finance, HR, Sales, and Marketing.

---

## 2. Why It Is Required
Overusing corporate buzzwords obscures clarity and impairs trust. The Jargon Dictionary educates professionals on when to use specific terms, the impact of overusing them, and plain-language alternatives.

---

## 3. Key Functionality & Specifications
- **308 Term Database**: Comprehensive collection of business buzzwords.
- **10 Detailed Breakdown Criteria**: `term`, `alternative`, `definition`, `usage`, `scenario`, `example`, `impact`, `generalVsPro`, `whenToUse`, and `howToUse`.
- **Lazy Loading Pagination**: Renders 10 terms initially, dynamically loading 10 more on scroll to ensure instant sub-millisecond screen initialization.
- **Real-Time Search Filter**: Instant query matching across terms, definitions, and alternatives.

---

## 4. How to Use Step-by-Step
1. Open **Corporate Jargon Dictionary** from Profile or Resources settings.
2. Type any term in the search bar (e.g. "Synergy", "Bandwidth", "Circle Back").
3. Tap on any card header to expand all 10 breakdown details.
4. Review recommended plain-language alternatives.
